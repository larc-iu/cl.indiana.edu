**ClingDing** is the computational linguistics colloquium at Indiana University.
We meet weekly during the semester for student talks, tutorials, invited speakers, and the occasional coffee hour.
All Indiana University affiliates are welcome regardless of department or degree program!

Please also consider joining our mailing list, [compling-l](mailto:compling-l-subscribe@indiana.edu).
To subscribe, send an empty email to <compling-l-subscribe@indiana.edu>.
