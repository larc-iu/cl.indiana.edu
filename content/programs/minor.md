# Minor in Computational Linguistics

The Minor in Computational Linguistics requires at least 15 credit hours.

## Introductory Courses (6 cr.)

* One of:
    * LING-L 103 Introduction to the Study of Language
    * LING-L 203 Introduction to Linguistic Analysis
* One of:
    * LING-L 307 Phonology
    * LING-L 310 Syntax

## Foundational Courses (9 cr.)

* One of:
    * INFO-I 101 Introduction to Informatics
    * INFO-I 210 Information Infrastructure I
    * INFO-H 101 Introduction to Informatics, Honors
    * INFO-H 210 Information Infrastructure I, Honors
* LING-L 235 Foundational Skills in Computational Linguistics
* LING-L 445 The Computer and Natural Language

## Electives

Additional courses, as needed to reach 15 credit hours:

* LING-L 245 Language and Computers
* LING-L 415 Corpus Linguistics
* Another related course approved by the Director of Undergraduate Studies

## Waivers

Students pursuing the B.A. in Linguistics may have the introductory language requirement waived, and LING-L 235 may be waived for students who already have programming skills.

For complete details, see [the undergraduate bulletin](https://bulletin.college.indiana.edu/programs/4255/CMPLNGMIN/).
