# 3+2 Joint B.S./M.S. in Computational Linguistics

This accelerated program allows students to earn both the B.S. and the M.S. in Computational Linguistics in five years, providing the skills needed for industry jobs in language technology.
The degree requires at least 133 credit hours in total, of which the undergraduate major accounts for at least 45, or 57 including the Outside Focal Area.

## Undergraduate Requirements

### Linguistics

* LING-L 103 Introduction to the Study of Language *or* LING-L 203 Introduction to Linguistic Analysis
* LING-L 306 Phonetics
* LING-L 307 Phonology
* LING-L 310 Syntax

### Computational

* LING-L 235 Foundational Skills in Computational Linguistics
* LING-L 245 Language and Computers
* LING-L 415 Corpus Linguistics
* LING-L 445 The Computer and Natural Language

### Computer Science

* One of:
    * CSCI-C 200 Introduction to Computers and Programming
    * CSCI-C 211 Introduction to Computer Science
    * CSCI-H 211 Introduction to Computer Science, Honors
* One of:
    * CSCI-C 212 Introduction to Software Systems
    * CSCI-H 212 Introduction to Software Systems, Honors
* One of:
    * CSCI-C 241 Discrete Structures for Computer Science
    * CSCI-H 241 Discrete Structures for Computer Science, Honors

### Math and Logic

* One mathematics course:
    * MATH-M 212 Calculus II
    * MATH-S 212 Honors Calculus II
* One statistics course:
    * MATH-M 365 Introduction to Probability and Statistics
    * STAT-S 350 Introduction to Statistical Inference
* One logic course:
    * COGS-Q 350 Mathematics and Logic for the Cognitive and Information Sciences
    * MATH-M 384 Logic
    * PHIL-P 250 Introductory Symbolic Logic

### Outside Focal Area

Either 12 credit hours in one of the disciplines below, or a minor offered by one of them:

* Cognitive science
* Computer science
* Informatics
* Mathematics
* Psychology
* A foreign language, which must differ from the language used to satisfy the General Education world languages requirement

## Admission to Graduate Status

Students must have completed at least 32 credit hours of core requirements toward the B.S. with a major GPA of at least 3.0.

Students are normally classified as undergraduates until the end of the first semester in which they have earned 120 or more credit hours toward graduation. During that semester, students in good standing apply to the University Graduate School and begin the transition to graduate status. At least 15 credit hours — normally no fewer than two semesters — must be completed while registered in graduate status.

## Graduate Requirements

The graduate portion consists of the requirements for the [M.S. in Computational Linguistics](ms.html), each completed with a grade of B- or higher. All University Graduate School policies and procedures apply.

## Internship

Students have the option of a summer internship, worth 2–3 credit hours, between the fourth and fifth years.

For complete details, see [the undergraduate bulletin](https://bulletin.college.indiana.edu/programs/4255/CMLNBSMSBS/) and [the University Graduate School bulletin](https://bulletins.iu.edu/iu/gradschool/2025-2026/programs/bloomington/linguistics/index.shtml).
