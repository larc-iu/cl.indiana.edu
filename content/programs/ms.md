# M.S. in Computational Linguistics

The **M.S. in Computational Linguistics** trains students to master cutting-edge language technologies, such as large language models and speech recognition systems, while also giving them a grounding in core areas of linguistic theory.

Our graduates are well employed in industry in roles with titles such as Analytical Linguist, Language Engineer, Data Scientist, and NLP Engineer. 
The program also serves as a strong foundation for those entering Ph.D. programs.

**No prior experience with computer programming or linguistics is required.** 
The curriculum is designed to accommodate students from diverse academic backgrounds, and students may also choose one of three tracks to specialize in:

* **Natural Language Processing** is for students who are interested in processing textual data.
* **Speech Processing** serves students who are most interested in models which process speech data.
* **Language Engineering** is suitable for students who want to deepen their knowledge of linguistic analysis.

## Requirements

These requirements apply to students entering the program in the 2026-2027 academic year and later.
Students who started before then follow the requirements that were in place when they began, and may choose to switch to these requirements instead.

The M.S. requires a minimum of 33 credit hours, or 36 if the statistics requirement is met through coursework.

### Core Courses (21 cr.)

* LING-L 541 Introductory Phonetics
* LING-L 545 Computation and Linguistic Analysis
* LING-L 565 Fundamentals of Speech Processing
* LING-L 605 Advanced Data Modeling for Computational Linguistics
* LING-L 645 Advanced Natural Language Processing
* LING-L 665 Applying Machine Learning Techniques in Computational Linguistics
* LING-L 715 Seminar in Computational Linguistics

### Tracks (9 cr.)

Students select one of the following three tracks.

**Natural Language Processing**

* One of:
    * LING-L 503 Introduction to Linguistics for Graduate Students
    * LING-L 542 Phonological Analysis
    * LING-L 543 Syntactic Analysis
    * LING-L 544 Morphological Analysis
    * LING-L 546 Semantics
    * LING-L 670 Language Typology
* LING-L 675 Advanced Machine Learning for Computational Linguistics
* One of:
    * CSCI-B 551 Elements of Artificial Intelligence
    * CSCI-B 555 Machine Learning

**Speech Processing**

* LING-L 635 Applications of Speech Processing
* LING-L 641 Advanced Phonetics
* One of:
    * ENGR-E 511 Machine Learning for Signal Processing
    * LING-L 675 Advanced Machine Learning for Computational Linguistics
    * CSCI-B 551 Elements of Artificial Intelligence
    * CSCI-B 555 Machine Learning

**Language Engineering**

* One of:
    * LING-L 542 Phonological Analysis
    * LING-L 543 Syntactic Analysis
    * LING-L 544 Morphological Analysis
    * LING-L 546 Semantics
    * LING-L 670 Language Typology
* LING-L 614 Alternative Syntactic Theories
* LING-L 615 Corpus Linguistics

A grade point average of 3.0 (B) must be achieved in the ten core and track courses.
